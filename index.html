About Viva Simulator

Understand Your Project.
Master Every Concept.

Most students can build a great project — but struggle to explain it under pressure during a viva. Viva Simulator bridges that gap. It reads your project materials, generates precise questions your examiner might ask, evaluates your answers, identifies where your understanding is weak, and trains you until you can explain every concept with clarity and confidence.

01
Project Understanding
Upload your project report, code, or documentation. The AI processes and indexes it into a searchable knowledge base specific to your work.
02
Technical Questioning
Receive targeted, project-aware viva questions generated from your own materials — not generic textbook questions.
03
Performance Improvement
Get instant feedback on each answer. Track your progress, revisit weak areas, and build confidence session by session.

Core Features

Everything you need to prepare
for your next viva.

Six focused capabilities, built specifically for project-based viva examinations.

01 — Upload
Project Upload & Analysis
Upload your project report, documentation, or code. The system parses, embeds, and indexes your content into a knowledge base ready for assessment.
02 — Generate
AI-Powered Viva Questions
Questions are generated directly from your project using a RAG pipeline — not from textbooks. Every question is relevant to what you built.
03 — Practise
Interactive Mock Viva
Simulate a real viva experience. Answer questions, navigate difficulty levels, and practice responding under realistic conditions.
04 — Evaluate
Answer Evaluation
Each answer is evaluated for accuracy, depth and clarity. You receive structured feedback showing exactly where your explanation fell short.
05 — Track
Performance Tracking
View your scores across sessions. See which concepts are mastered and which need more attention — session by session, topic by topic.
06 — Adapt
Personalized Practice
The system identifies your knowledge gaps and re-focuses your practice sessions on weak areas, adapting difficulty as your mastery grows.
